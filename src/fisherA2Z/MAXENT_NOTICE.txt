The continuous maximum-entropy reconstruction functions in nz_moments.py
are adapted from the user-supplied pymaxent_jax.py (PyMaxEnt, version 1.0.0),
which identifies Tony Saad and Giovanna Ruai as authors and declares MIT.
The pdf_maxent-style wrappers follow the user's supplied pdf_maxent.py.
The Fisher interfaces, input-moment integration and validation are additions.
